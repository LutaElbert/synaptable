## Video direction

- Palette: SynapTable near-white canvas and panel surfaces, graphite ink, muted gray chrome, and one violet accent, exactly as mapped in `frame.md`. Display, body, and caption type all use the mapped Inter roles.
- Motion grammar: the real 1920×1080 recording is the hero. Use smooth long-tail settles (`power3` by default), deliberate source-time cuts, and VO-paced overlays. Reveal only the phrase or callout being spoken; never dump all copy at the opening.
- Rhythm: Frames 1–3 advance through the workflow; Frame 4 deliberately transfers visual weight from the edited table to the benefit line; Frame 5 is the calm held close. Camera motion stops before every read. No lazy breathing.
- Framing: preserve the product's readable UI, stay inside the top ~83% caption-safe field, and use only modest crops with enough source resolution. The first rendered frame must visibly be the empty SynapTable project.
- Negative list: no invented interface, fake metrics, generic feature-card montage, purple-blue AI haze, bouncy entrances, front-loaded slideshow motion, or independently floating screensaver elements.
