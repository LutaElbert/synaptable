## Video direction

- **Product truth:** every behavioral claim is shown with the real SynapTable interface. Motion may crop, reframe, highlight, or annotate the capture, but it must never reconstruct or fabricate product behavior.
- **Reference translation:** borrow the calm editorial rhythm of Anthropic's official Claude walkthrough—one centered product surface, large copy only when it adds meaning, decisive reframing into the active control, and a still read after each result. Keep SynapTable's own indigo, off-white, ink, Inter typography, hairlines, and square geometry; do not copy Claude's terracotta palette or serif identity.
- **Motion grammar:** the camera follows intent. Use one nested media camera wrapper per frame, smooth long-tail settles (`power3`/`expo.out`), and reveal each overlay on the matching spoken cue across the back half of the frame. Real footage supplies cursor and UI actions; never add a fake cursor over recorded interaction.
- **Rhythm:** RECOGNIZE (1) → SETTLE (2) → DISCOVER (3) → TRANSFORM (4) → TRAVEL (5) → VERIFY (6) → HOLD (7). Frames 2, 6, and 7 are deliberate breathers; Frame 4 is the visual peak.
- **Continuity:** transitions preserve the product surface as the visual anchor. Motion uses the approved sequence: zoom-through → blur crossfade → leftward push → leftward push → crossfade → zoom-through. Keep all load-bearing UI and overlay copy in the top ~83% caption-safe region.
- **Negative list:** no floating SaaS cards, decorative particles, fake terminal theater, AI gradients, browser chrome, unmotivated 3D tilt, lazy breathing, repeated bounce, screensaver drift, or front-loaded slideshow entrances.
